ECG Signal Classification  

Project Background 

Electrocardiography (ECG) is one of the most widely used diagnostic tools for assessing cardiac health and detecting abnormalities in heart function. Analysis of ECG recordings can help identify a variety of cardiovascular conditions, including arrhythmias, myocardial infarction, and conduction disorders. However, ECG signals are time-series data that can be complex to analyze due to signal variability, noise, and the large number of diagnostic categories. 

This project focuses on the PTB-XL dataset, a large-scale publicly available ECG database containing 12-lead ECG recordings together with expert diagnostic annotations. The goal is to investigate how artificial intelligence can be used to automatically classify ECG recordings into different diagnostic categories. Students will explore methods for preprocessing ECG signals, extracting meaningful information from time-series data, and developing machine learning models capable of supporting automated cardiac diagnosis. 

Project Objectives  

The student will investigate and develop a solution for automatic classification of ECG signals using artificial intelligence. The project includes the following objectives:  

Study the characteristics and clinical relevance of ECG signals.  

Explore the structure and annotation scheme of the PTB-XL dataset.  

Investigate preprocessing techniques for cleaning and preparing ECG recordings.  

Examine methods for handling multivariate time-series biomedical data.  

Design, implement, and compare machine learning and/or deep learning models for ECG classification.  

Develop a complete classification pipeline from raw ECG signals to diagnostic predictions.  

Evaluate model performance using appropriate validation strategies and performance metrics.  

Analyze the strengths and limitations of the proposed solution.  

Assess the potential of AI-based ECG analysis for supporting clinical decision-making and healthcare applications.  

 

Dataset  

The project will use the PTB-XL ECG Dataset, a large publicly available collection of annotated 12-lead ECG recordings. The dataset contains thousands of clinical ECG records together with diagnostic labels assigned by medical experts, making it well suited for machine learning and deep learning applications in biomedical signal processing.  

 

Dataset link: https://physionet.org/content/ptb-xl/1.0.3/
